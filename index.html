How it works

Five questions, in the order you need them.

PreEvac watches authoritative disaster feeds continuously, works out whether an event actually reaches you, decides how serious that is, and tells you what to do.

01

Where am I?

Your position is resolved on the device. Only the coordinates needed for matching are sent, and no location history is kept.

02

What is happening?

Earthquakes, floods, cyclones, wildfires, storms, volcanoes and more, pulled from the agencies that issue the warnings.

03

How close is it?

Each event gets an impact radius from its own physics. A magnitude 7 quake is felt 450 km away; a wildfire hotspot threatens ten.

04

How serious is it?

Severity is decided by fixed rules from verified data, then decayed by distance. An official warning is a floor and is never quietly downgraded.

05

What do I do?

Human-written guidance for every disaster type, stored on your device so it is readable with no signal at all.

+

What is happening around me?

A regional view of places nearby currently going through a disaster, scored by how serious it is where it is happening.

What PreEvac will not do

It never invents a disaster.

Alert text is written from verified facts by fixed templates. Where an AI model is used, it is handed those facts and an already-decided severity and asked only to phrase them. Every response is checked for invented figures, predicted disasters, made-up emergency numbers and fabricated evacuation routes, and anything short of a clean pass is discarded.

1. Official emergency authority
2. Scientific agencies: USGS, NASA, national meteorological services
3. GDACS
4. Commercial weather providers
5. AI interpretation, which can never outrank any of the above

Every alert in the app names its source, its event ID and its timestamp, and links straight through to the authority that issued it.

Install

Two minutes, no account.

PreEvac is not on the Play Store yet, so Android will ask you to confirm the install. That is normal for an app installed directly.

Download PreEvac APK
  1. Download the APK using the button above.
  2. Open it from your notification shade or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs from this source when Android asks. You are granting your browser permission to install, once.
  4. Open PreEvac and allow location when it explains why. You can skip this and add places manually instead.
  5. Allow notifications so alerts can reach you when the app is closed.
Presenting it to someone? Settings › Demo mode moves the app to a place that is genuinely going through a disaster right now, chosen from the live feed, and raises the alert on your device so you can show it arriving. Nothing about it is simulated except where the app believes it is standing.

PreEvac complements official emergency warning systems. It depends on upstream data availability, satellite observations, government agencies, connectivity and mobile network delivery, so it cannot guarantee that an alert arrives in time. Always follow instructions from local authorities.
